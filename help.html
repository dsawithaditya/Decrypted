Help & User Guide

Everything you need to know about checking queue times, claiming express passes, ordering room food delivery, and managing mess operations.

01 · Student Guide

Using the Student Portal

⚡

FINDING THE BEST COUNTER

Open the Student View on your phone. The gold banner at the top automatically points you to the open counter with the lowest wait time.

🎫

CROSS-ACCESS EXPRESS PASS

When national lines exceed 80%, a glowing banner appears. Tap Claim Express Pass to get a 15-minute token routing you to the least crowded International counter.

🔔

"NOTIFY ME WHEN FREE"

Studying in your hostel room? Tap the alert button to receive an audio chime and notification when mess crowd drops below 50%.

🛏️

DIRECT-TO-ROOM DELIVERY

If you have a high fever, mobility injury, or quarantine, fill out the Room Delivery form. Select Light Sick Diet (Khichdi/Soup) or Standard Thali.

🔒

4-DIGIT SECURITY OTP

Each delivery generates a unique 4-digit OTP. Keep it on your screen and share it with the runner when your food arrives to verify delivery.

🌟

EARNING KARMA POINTS

Volunteer as a Hostel Peer Buddy to carry a meal box to an unwell student in your hostel block and earn +10 Mess Karma Points.

02 · Staff Operations

Operating the Staff Console

🔑

STAFF ACCESS

Open the Staff Console from the navbar. Enter default staff PIN 1234 or sign in with your staff email address.

🎛️

QUEUE CONTROLS

Adjust line sizes using sliders or + / − buttons. Check the Sold Out box to immediately divert students from counters that have run out of food.

🚨

KITCHEN EARLY WARNING

If any dish rating falls below 2.5★ or receives 3+ complaint tags (Cold, Salt Issue), an automatic red alert sounds so chefs can correct the recipe.

03 · Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Why does the Standby counter open at 50% instead of 80%?

Opening standby at 50% crowd provides an early buffer before lines back up into the dining hall. By preparing at 45% and opening at 50%, queues never reach dangerous congestion levels.

How does the 75% Smart Overflow Cap protect International counters?

International counters have smaller capacities (15 people). If cross-access diverted all National students at once, International counters would instantly flood. The 75% cap pauses routing to any International counter that reaches 75% capacity.

How do I switch the language to Kannada or Hindi?

Click the EN / हिं / ಕನ್ button located in the top navigation bar. It cycles smoothly between English, Hindi, and Kannada on all screens.

Does the app work offline if campus Wi-Fi drops?

Yes! The system includes an automatic offline resilience engine using localStorage and BroadcastChannel. It continues displaying cached counter counts and queue statuses with a freshness warning chip.
